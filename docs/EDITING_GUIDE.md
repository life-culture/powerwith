# Editing your website

This guide is for Sophia. It covers every change you can make yourself.

## Sign in

1. Go to **https://life-culture.github.io/powerwith/admin/**
2. Choose **Sign In with GitHub** if you see it. Otherwise choose **Sign In with Token**, follow the link it gives you to GitHub, create the token, copy it and paste it in. Your browser remembers you after that.

The editor works on a phone too.

## Find the words you want to change

- **Pages** holds every page. Home, the Introduction landing page, Pre-order, Thank you, Privacy and Page not found.
- **Site settings** holds the navigation, the footer, and the form messages.

Open a page. Its **Sections** are listed in the order they appear. Click a section to open it.

## Change text

Click into any field and type. In paragraphs you can make words **bold**, *italic*, or a link with the small toolbar. Delete text the normal way.

## Publish

Press **Save**. Your change is live in about two minutes. Refresh the site to see it.

## Show, hide and reorder sections

- **Hide a section.** Open it and switch off **Show this section on the page**. Save. Switch it back on to show it again. Nothing is deleted.
- **Reorder.** Drag a section by its handle, or use the arrows on it.
- **Duplicate.** Open the menu on a section and choose **Duplicate**, for example to add a second quote band.
- **Add.** Press **Add** under the sections and choose a type.

Lists inside sections work the same way. Principles, the "What you will learn to do" list, questions, retailer buttons, footer links.

## Change a background

Each section has a **Background color** choice. Pick one of the six brand grounds. The text and button colors switch for you so everything stays readable.

## Replace an image

Open the section, click the image, and upload a new file. Large photos are fine. They are resized and compressed for you. Then update the **Image description** so people using screen readers know what it shows.

## Search titles and descriptions

Each page has **Search and sharing** at the top. The page title and search description live there.

## Undo a change

Every save is kept. To bring back an earlier version, ask Benjamin, or open the page's history on GitHub (the repository's file history) and restore it. You can also simply retype the old words and save.

## Words in square brackets

Text like `[Years in practice]` marks something still to supply. Replace it or delete it. A button whose link is in square brackets shows faded until you add a real link.

## Please leave alone

The two **form destination** fields in Site settings. Benjamin set them, and they connect your forms to EmailOctopus.
